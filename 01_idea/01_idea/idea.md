## Working Title
WANDR
## The idea in one sentence
WANDR helps students and young adults discover local events and activities that match their interests when they are looking for something interesting to do in their free time.

## Who it is for
- User group: Students and young adults aged 18–25 who want to explore their city and discover new   experiences.
- Where I can find five of them: Student residences and friends in Budapest.
- Device and situation: Mobile phone, when deciding what to do after classes, during weekends or whenever they have free time.

## The problem (my hypothesis)
I believe students and young adults miss interesting events and activities because information is scattered across different websites, social media platforms and apps.
They often spend too much time searching for events, struggle to find activities that match their interests or simply don't know what is happening around them.
I will know I am right if, during interviews, they tell me about missing interesting events, struggling to find activities or spending too much time searching across different platforms.

## Platform and Why
- Platform: Mobile application (iOS and Android).
- Why: Students mainly use their phones when looking for things to do. A mobile app makes it easier to discover nearby events, explore different categories and save interesting activities for later.

## Out of scope
- Organising private events or managing group invitations.
- Social networking, messaging or creating a separate community platform.
