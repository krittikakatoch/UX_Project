### Competitors

### 1.Erasmus Life Budapest . https://erasmuslifebudapest.com/
- What I take from it: promoting student-focused parties, trips and social activities through a dedicated community.
- What I would avoid: depending heavily on Instagram and social media instead of having one organized platform for event discovery.
### 2.Clic .https://www.letsclic.com/
- What I take from it: connecting people through small group chats before they attend an event.
- What I would avoid: making social matching the main focus instead of event discovery.
### 3.Blipest .https://blipest.hu/en
- What I take from it: combining event categories and map-based browsing to show what's happening around Budapest.
- What I would avoid: limiting the experience to browsing events without stronger social features for students.
### 4.Fever .https://feverup.com/
- What I take from it: combining event discovery, detailed descriptions, prices and booking options in one place.
- What I would avoid: focusing mainly on paid experiences and ticketed events while overlooking free or student-friendly activities.

### Patterns

### 1.DICE "Event discovery visual style" .https://dice.fm/
- The pattern: organising entertainment into clear categories such as Movies, Events, Dining and Activities.
- Why it fits my app: users can quickly find the type of activity they want without searching through everything.
### 2.Eventbrite "Search and filtering" .https://www.eventbrite.com/
- The pattern: filtering events by date, location and category.
- Why it fits my app: students can find activities that match their schedule, interests and budget.
### 3.Fever "Image-led content cards" .https://feverup.com/
- The pattern: using large images and visual cards to display events and experiences.
- Why it fits my app: users can get a feel for an activity before deciding whether they want to attend.
