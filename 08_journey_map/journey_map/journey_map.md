# Worst Stage: Book

The **Book** stage is the most frustrating part of Dóri's journey.

After finding an event she likes, she may struggle to locate the correct booking link or get redirected to confusing websites. This can make it difficult to complete the booking and sometimes causes her to stop trying.

**Research evidence:**
- said, "We couldn't find the link." 
- sometimes stopped trying because of unclear booking links and confusing website navigation. 

**Design opportunity:**
Make booking links easy to find, clearly labelled and reliable, so users can move from discovering an event to accessing tickets without unnecessary confusion.
