# User Persona

## 01. Name and situation

**Dóri, 21 · University student · Budapest**

Dóri enjoys attending concerts, parties and social events. She discovers events through Instagram and event websites, often switching between platforms to find information before deciding what to attend.

## 02. Goal

Find interesting events that match her interests and easily access all the details she needs before deciding to attend.

## 03. Behaviours

- **Discovers through social media:** Uses Instagram and social media pages to find upcoming events .
- **Compares event details:** Checks prices, dates, times and locations before deciding whether to attend .
- **Uses multiple sources:** Switches between Instagram, event websites and search engines to find additional information .

## 04. Frustrations

- **Scattered information:** Has to visit multiple platforms to find complete event details .
- **Ticket uncertainty:** Encounters sold-out events and sometimes finds tickets too expensive .
- **Booking difficulties:** Experiences unclear booking links, unexpected redirects and language barriers .

## 05. Context

- **Device:** iphone .
- **Time:** Searches for events when planning her free time .
- **Place:** Uses social media and event websites to discover events .
- **Connectivity:** Uses online platforms, social media and search engines to find event information .
