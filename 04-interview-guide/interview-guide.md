# Interview Guide · 15–20 minutes

**Topic:** How people discover, choose and book events in their city.

### 01 · Introduction
Thanks for your time. I'm studying how people discover events, decide which ones to attend and book tickets. There are no right or wrong answers.

### 02 · Consent
May I take notes during our conversation? I won't write down your name, and you can stop at any time.

### 03 · Context
**Q1.** How often do you attend events such as concerts, parties, festivals or exhibitions?

**Q2.** How do you usually find out about events happening in your city?

### 04 · Recent experience
**Q3.** Tell me about the last time you looked for an event to attend. How did you find it?

**Q4.** Which apps, websites or other sources did you use?

*Probe: Can you show me how you found the event?*

### 05 · Decision-making
**Q5.** What makes you decide whether an event is worth attending?

**Q6.** What information do you usually look for before deciding to buy a ticket?

*Probe: Is there anything you usually struggle to find?*

### 06 · Difficulties
**Q7.** Have you ever missed an event because you didn't know about it or found out too late?

**Q8.** What is the most frustrating experience you've had while finding or booking event tickets?

*Probe: Why? What happened then?*

### 07 · Workarounds
**Q9.** Have you ever abandoned a ticket purchase? What made you change your mind?

**Q10.** What do you usually do when you cannot find an event or information you're looking for?

### 08 · Ideal experience
**Q11.** Is there anything you wish was easier when discovering or booking events?

### 09 · Closing question
Is there anything else about finding, choosing or attending events that I should have asked but didn't?

### 10 · Thank you
Thank you for sharing your experience. May I contact you again in a few weeks to test a prototype?

---

### Interview Probes

- Why?
- Tell me more.
- What happened then?
