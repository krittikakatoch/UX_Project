# P3 · Week 2 · Library · 15 min

Consent: notes and recording: yes

About P3: 21, student, Iphone, attends events often, uses websites and Instagram to discover events

- **Q1 · asked:** How often do you attend events such as concerts, parties, festivals or exhibitions?
- **said:** "Often, especially when there's something interesting happening."
- **observation:** Regularly attends events and actively looks for things to do.

- **Q2 · asked:** How do you usually find out about events happening in your city?
- **said:** "Through websites and Instagram pages."
- **did:** Browsed Instagram pages and event websites to discover upcoming events.

- **Q3 · asked:** Tell me about the last time you looked for an event to attend. How did you find it?
- **said:** Found an event through an Instagram page and checked the website for more details.
- **did:** Switched between Instagram and the event website to find information.

- **Q4 · asked:** Which apps, websites or other sources did you use?
- **said:** "Instagram and websites."
- **did:** Used Instagram and website to explore events.
- **observation:** Relies on social media for discovery and websites for event information.

- **Q5 · asked:** What makes you decide whether an event is worth attending?
- **said:** "The type of people, vibe, music and timing."
- **preferred:** Events with a good atmosphere, music and an audience that matches his interests.
- **observation:** The social atmosphere is an important factor in choosing an event.

- **Q6 · asked:** What information do you usually look for before deciding to buy a ticket?
- **said:** "Price, place and date."
- **did:** Checked the event location, date and ticket price before deciding.
- **preferred:** Clear event details without having to search through multiple pages.

- **Q7 · asked:** Have you ever missed an event because you didn't know about it or found out too late?
- **said:** "Yes, sometimes I find out about events after they are already happening."
- **observation:** Discovering events through social media can result in missing events that are not promoted directly to him.

- **Q8 · asked:** What is the most frustrating experience you've had while finding or booking event tickets?
- **said:** "Sometimes you have to go through different websites to find the information."
- **struggled with:** Finding complete information about events across different platforms.
- **observation:** Switching between platforms makes event discovery less convenient.

- **Q9 · asked:** Have you ever abandoned a ticket purchase? What made you change your mind?
- **said:** "Yes, when the price was too expensive or when I wasn't sure about the event."
- **did:** Decided not to purchase a ticket when the event didn't seem worth the price.
- **observation:** Ticket price and uncertainty about the event influence purchase decisions.

- **Q10 · asked:** What do you usually do when you cannot find an event or information you're looking for?
- **said:** "I check Instagram pages or search on Google."
- **workaround:** Uses social media and search engines to find missing event information.

- **Q11 · asked:** Is there anything you wish was easier when discovering or booking events?
- **said:** "Finding events that match my vibe and getting notifications about them."
- **preferred:** Personalised recommendations and notifications about upcoming events.
- **observation:** Personalisation could help him discover relevant events more quickly.

- **Closing question · asked:** Is there anything else about finding, choosing or attending events that I should have asked but didn't?
- **said:** Nothing additional was mentioned.

## Overall observations

- **observation:** Frequently attends events and discovers them through websites and Instagram pages.
- **observation:** Uses Instagram to find events.
- **observation:** The audience, atmosphere, music and timing influence event selection.
- **observation:** Price, location and date are the most important information before booking.
- **workaround:** Switches between Instagram, websites and Google to find event information.
- **preferred:** Personalised recommendations, notifications and complete event information in one place.
- **surprised me:** The participant considers the type of people attending an event as important as the event itself.
- **UX insight:** Frequent event-goers need a platform that helps them discover events matching their social preferences while making event details and booking easily accessible.
