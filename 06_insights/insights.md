# Insights

## Insight 1 · Event discovery

**We saw:** 3 of 3 participants (P1, P2, P3) used multiple platforms, including Instagram, websites and event apps, to discover events.

**Because:** Event information is spread across different platforms, making it difficult to find and compare events in one place.

**So the design must:** Bring event discovery and essential event information together in one place.

## Insight 2 · Event atmosphere

**We saw:** 2 of 3 participants (P2, P3) considered the atmosphere, audience and music when deciding which events to attend.

**Because:** The event title and category alone do not communicate the experience or type of crowd they can expect.

**So the design must:** Show the atmosphere, audience and music through relevant visuals and descriptions.

## Insight 3 · Booking confidence

**We saw:** 3 of 3 participants (P1, P2, P3) encountered unclear or incomplete event information, including ticket availability, prices and booking details.

**Because:** Missing information makes it difficult to compare events and feel confident before booking.

**So the design must:** Display clear pricing, ticket availability, location, dates and booking information.

## My hypothesis from Step 1

**Right about:** Users need an easier way to discover Budapest events and access event information without switching between multiple platforms.

**Wrong about:** I initially assumed that easier event discovery was the main problem, but research showed that users also need clear event details, including prices, availability, location and booking information, before deciding to attend.
