# Benchmark

Main task I tested: Find an interesting event in Budapest that I could attend.

Device and width: iPhone 16, Chrome device mode.

## A: DICE

Taps to goal: 6

Works:
- Strong visual identity illustrations
- Event discovery image led event cards 
- Minimal interface simplicity

Fails:
- Ticket accessibility
- Limited Event Selection
- Poor Personalization

Review quotes:
- "This app is so full of friction. You have to go through multiple steps just to access tickets to a gig that you've already purchased. Then, if you want to share them, it cannot handle a phone number saved in standard format. I literally had to update my husband's phone number in my phone contacts just to send him a ticket. Madness."
- "It's a ticketing app. It does what it needs to, but I begrudge its existence. I'd rather not need to install yet another app for everything in life. This could've been an email, a PDF, a website, or some combination of all three. The app puts a lot of focus on automatically adding you to as many marketing lists as possible. The UI is a mixed bag. The notification settings in specific are terrible - they don't use any words to show whether you're subscribed or not, just a bad toggle."

## B: Fever

Taps to goal: 7

Works:
- Event detail pages
- Booking flow
- Clear event cards

Fails:
- Limited filtering
- difficult to navigate
- Availability uncertainty

Review quotes:
- "Be careful when purchasing tickets here. The refund process is unclear, it’s been over 20 days, and I still haven’t received my money back. Their customer service is unhelpful and never provides any updates on the status of the refund. You might want to think twice before buying tickets through this app. Their customer service also uses AI, so I never get a clear or definitive answer. It’s incredibly annoying and frustrating, I want my money back."
- "DON’T PURCHASE TICKETS. Customer service is terrible. The AI bot drags things out, and IF you reach a person, they aren’t helpful. I accidentally bought Dec 17 tickets instead of Sept 27. I contacted them immediately after. Same event, same price, both events weeks away. They refused to change the date, when I asked for a refund so I could repurchase tickets, they told me all sales were final and offered a voucher for less than what I paid."

## C: District

Taps to goal: 7

Works:
- Visual interface
- Personalised discovery
- Event categorisation

Fails:
- Limited event information
- Users may find it difficult to save and organise events for later
- Overwhelming discovery

Review quotes:
- "Extremely disappointing and unacceptable service from the District Movie app! There is no option to cancel a booking, no refund facility, and not even an option to reschedule the show. Customers are left helpless after making a booking, with no flexibility or proper support. This is nothing short of exploitation of customers. I strongly condemn this poor service."
- "I have faced very bad experience with this App support. there is a possibility of rescheduling the ticket but stating that it is not possible. so they don't know what they have mentioned in the app. you have a technical error but you are covering it which is not good. I sincerely not encourage this app support anymore. please people take this seriously. thank you."

## Patterns to reuse
- DICE: Playful illustrations, stickers and strong visual identity.
- Fever: Visually rich event cards and category-based discovery.
- District: Useful event categorization and ticket information.
## Gaps nobody solves
- Local discovery: Better coverage of smaller events and activities.
- Personalization: Recommendations based on interests, budget and availability.
- Event transparency: Clear information about costs, location, age restrictions and event details.
